Goa


Goa is India's smallest state and most popular beach destination, located on the southwestern coast. Known for its golden beaches, Portuguese heritage, vibrant nightlife, and delicious seafood, Goa offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. Whether you want sunbathing, water sports, temple visits, or partying, Goa has something for everyone.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach - The bustling beach with water sports and nightlife
  2. Fort Aguada - The stunning 17th-century Portuguese fort with lighthouse
  3. Dudhsagar Falls - The majestic 310-meter waterfall on Mandovi River
  4. Old Goa Churches - UNESCO world heritage basilicas from 16th century
  5. Palolem Beach - The serene crescent-shaped beach in South Goa

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry - The signature coconut curry with fresh fish
  • Bebinca - Traditional 7-layered dessert with egg and coconut
  • Pork Vindaloo - Spicy Portuguese-inspired curry with vinegar
  • Prawn Balchão - Tangy spicy prawn pickle with shrimp
  • Feni - Local cashew liquor unique to Goa
