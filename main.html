Manali


Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh known for its snow-capped mountains, scenic valleys, adventure sports, and pleasant weather. It is a popular destination for nature lovers and travelers.

  1. Solang Valley - Famous for adventure activities.
  2. Rohtang Pass - Offers breathtaking mountain views.
  3. Hadimba Temple - An ancient temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Manu Temple - A significant religious site in Manali.
  5. Old Manali - Known for its cafes and charming atmosphere.
  • Siddu - A traditional Himachali bread.
  • Thukpa - A flavorful noodle soup.
  • Trout Fish - A popular local delicacy.
  • Babru - A Himachali snack similar to kachori.
  • Mittha - A sweet rice dish.
