Jaipur, Rajasthan


Jaipur, known as the "Pink City," is the vibrant capital of Rajasthan, India. This historic city fascinates visitors with its stunning palaces, intricate forts, bustling markets, and warm hospitality. Jaipur perfectly blends royal heritage with modern charm, offering an unforgettable experience of Indian culture, architecture, and cuisine.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Hawa Mahal - The iconic Pink Palace with 953 tiny windows
  2. Amber Fort - A magnificent 16th-century fort with stunning architecture
  3. City Palace - A grand royal complex overlooking Lake Palace
  4. Jantar Mantar - An ancient astronomical observatory with unique instruments
  5. Jal Mahal - The beautiful Water Palace situated in Mansingh Lake

Must-Try Foods

  • Dal Baati Churma - The signature Rajasthani dish with lentils and sweet crumble
  • Ghevar - A traditional sweet honeycake made with ghee
