Kerala


Kerala, known as "God's Own Country," is a beautiful tropical state in southern India renowned for its lush green landscapes, tranquil backwaters, and rich biodiversity. This serene destination offers a perfect mix of nature, culture, and relaxation with its famous houseboat cruises, aromatic spice plantations, and pristine beaches.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Alleppey Backwaters - The most serene waterway experience in India
  2. Munnar Tea Gardens - Famous rolling hills covered with tea plantations
  3. Wayanad Forest - A wildlife-rich sanctuary with ancient caves
  4. Varapuzha Bridge - The longest bridge over Kerala's backwaters
  5. Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary - Pristine natural habitat for migratory birds

Must-Try Foods

  • Appam with Stew - The traditional Kerala breakfast dish
  • Parotta with Beef - Most popular snack in Kerala restaurants
  • Sadya - A festive vegetarian feast served on banana leaf
  • Kerala Porridge - Rich coconut-based traditional dessert
  • Fish Moorukun - Fresh catch cooked in spicy coconut gravy
