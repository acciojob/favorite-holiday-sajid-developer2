Mumbai, Maharashtra


Mumbai, formerly known as Bombay, is the bustling financial capital of India and the City of Dreams. This vibrant coastal metropolis offers a thrilling mix of colonial architecture, modern skyscrapers, diverse cuisine, and the glamour of Bollywood. From serene beaches to iconic landmarks, Mumbai captures the energy and spirit of India.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Gateway of India - The iconic 26-meter arch built in 1924
  2. Marine Drive - The stunning 8-km promenade along the Arabian Sea
  3. Elephanta Caves - Ancient Sanskara temple caves from the 5th century
  4. Siddhivinayak Temple - The revered Ganesha shrine with millions of devotees
  5. Haji Ali Dargah - The beautiful mosque and shrine on a rocky island

Must-Try Foods

  • Vada Pav - Mumbai's signature street snack with spicy potato
  • Pani Puri - Explosive tangy galli food with flavored water
  • Bhel Puri - Crispy chaat mixture with puffed rice and spices
  • Misal Pav - Fiery spicy curry served with bread
  • Kulumbai - Refreshing coconut water drink from beach stalls
